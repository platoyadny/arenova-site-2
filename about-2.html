Our History

Arenova was founded with a clear mission: to bring exceptional design and construction solutions to Saint Petersburg. Our work combines architectural expertise, structural integrity, and aesthetic vision.

Over the years, we’ve delivered a wide range of residential and commercial projects, always with a focus on detail, quality, and client satisfaction.

With a team of dedicated professionals, we continue to expand our portfolio, shaping the future of design and construction in the region.
